### 💼 Engineering Experience & Systems Architecture

Throughout my 8+ years in the industry, I have spearheaded the development of robust, scalable platforms across multiple domains.

- 🧠 **Agentic AI & LLM Systems:** Architecting autonomous multi-agent pipelines using **LangChain, LangGraph, and CrewAI**. Expertise in **RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation)**, **Vector Databases (Milvus, Pinecone)**, and **LLM Fine-Tuning** (Llama, Mistral). I build **Autonomous SDR Agents**, advanced lead-scoring algorithms, and intelligent chatbots that dynamically parse PDFs, Excel, and live CRM data.
- 🏗️ **Frontend Leadership:** Leading frontend architecture for enterprise-grade SaaS products. I specialize in **Atomic Design Systems**, state management, and performance tuning using **React, Angular, Next.js, and TypeScript**.
- ⚙️ **Backend & Microservices:** Building intelligent, highly scalable microservices with **Node.js & Express**. I design databases using **PostgreSQL, MongoDB, and DynamoDB**.
- 🔌 **Enterprise ERP & Data Pipelines:** Expert in integrating complex ERP/CRM systems like **Salesforce and SAP**. I also manage ELT (Extract, Load, Transform) data synchronization pipelines using **Airbyte** and AWS Redshift for massive-scale analytics.
